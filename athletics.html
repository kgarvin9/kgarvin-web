Highlights

Private lessons

Kristen works with youth, middle school, and high school players on the fundamentals that translate to real games — stick skills, footwork, reads, and confidence. Sessions are tailored to wherever the player is right now.

Stick Skills

Passing, catching, weak-hand reps, and cleaner ball control at game speed.

Footwork & Dodging

First steps, change of direction, approach angles, and balance through contact.

Field IQ

Spacing, transition reads, two-way effort, and simpler decisions under pressure.

Confidence

Session structure that meets players where they are and builds from there.
