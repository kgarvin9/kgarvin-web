Training

Private lessons

Kristen offers private and small-group lacrosse lessons for youth, middle school, and high school players. Sessions focus on fundamentals that hold up in real games, better decision-making in space, and more confidence on both ends of the field.

Stick Skills Under Pressure

Passing, catching, weak-hand reps, quick exchanges, and cleaner ball control when the drill speed rises.

Footwork & Dodging

Explosive first steps, change-of-direction work, approach angles, and better balance entering or exiting contact.

Midfield Reads

Spacing, transition lanes, two-way effort, and the simple decisions that help players stop overthinking live reps.

Confidence Building

Session structure that gives younger players clear wins while still challenging high school athletes at game tempo.

Youth players

Fundamentals, comfort with the stick, and athletic movement patterns.

Middle school

Skill polish, confidence, and position-specific repetition.

High school

Cleaner details, faster decisions, and next-level habits.
